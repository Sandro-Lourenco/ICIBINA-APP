# ICIBINA — 21st Design Context — V5.8

Projection of `design-system/integrative-medicine/MASTER.md`; the Master remains authoritative.

## Product
Professional integrative-medicine education platform: medical editorial, scientific calm, natural precision, modern academic and human warmth.

## Four canonical surface profiles

### Editorial Light
Public, course detail, checkout and auth. Light editorial composition, professional photography, strong trust hierarchy, low-to-medium motion.

### Student Immersive
Student dashboard, lesson player, library and learning paths. Petroleum-dark premium learning environment, contextual photography, selective Source Serif 4 headings, strong progress hierarchy and controlled Motion choreography.

### Operational Light
Teacher dashboard and course builder. Light, productive, medium/high density, focused on authoring and workflow.

### Operational Neutral
Admin/operations/audit/observability. Dense, explicit, data-first, minimal motion.

## Foundations
- Light primary `#1C6254`; canvas `#F8FAF7`; surface `#FFFFFF`; text `#12211D`.
- Student Immersive: background `#071613`, surface `#0D211D`, foreground `#F5FAF7`, primary `#8AD6C0`, accent `#D9B66E`.
- Inter for UI; Source Serif 4 only for controlled editorial headings.
- Radius 8/12/16/24; elevation subtle and purposeful.
- WCAG 2.2 AA, visible focus, keyboard operation and reduced motion mandatory.
- Nature is atmosphere/context, never evidence of medical efficacy.

## Component policy
Reuse local primitives first. Search 21st before hand-writing a generic missing pattern. Adapt every candidate to local tokens, semantics, accessibility, states, responsiveness and dependency policy.

## Motion policy
CSS for simple feedback; Motion for state, layout continuity, presence, reorder/drag and deliberate student choreography. Motion+ is optional.

## Review
Substantial UI requires `21st review` plus project visual/a11y checks. Never silently redesign brand foundations.
